Lead with the counted outcome, not the model

The headline probability is now a count over real prices rather than a
simulation.  For every entry day in the downloaded history the same contract
(strike, breakeven and horizon held fixed) is settled on that window's real
exit, and the page reports how many of those windows actually made money.

  * omega/sim.py: add hist_distributions_full(), which rolls the study over the
    whole two years we download instead of the 126-day window -- the sample goes
    from ~245 to ~495 windows, so the Wilson interval tightens and no single
    stretch of the calendar drives the answer.  add hist_outcome(), which counts
    the exact trade: finished beyond breakeven, finished in the money, touched
    the level, and the real dollar P&L of every instance.

  * Payoff is computed with the contract's moneyness held fixed, not its dollar
    strike.  A strike that happens to sit deep in the money today used to look
    as if it won almost every historical window (profit factor 368).  Scaling by
    spot/entry makes the result comparable to today's premium and the profit
    factors are now 0.17-1.05, which is the honest picture for long options.

  * omega/engine.py: pop/pop_lo/pop_hi/p_itm/p_touch now come from the count.
    The model's numbers are kept and renamed model_pop/model_itm/model_touch so
    the UI can show them side by side instead of conflating the two.  hist_edge
    is now history minus model (it previously compared a number to itself).
    omega is scored on the counted payoff first, with the modelled EV as a
    fallback.

  * index.html: every surface says whether a number was counted or modelled.
    Strike rows show wins/total and real average P&L, horizon and pick cards
    lead with the count, and the "why" block splits into a counted panel and a
    clearly-labelled model reference.

Verified: 15/15 tickers, 0 errors, cycle 53.9s, and for every pick on the page
hist_win_count / hist_n reproduces pop exactly.
